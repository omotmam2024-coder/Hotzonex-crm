# Hotzonex CRM — Staff Guide

A quick guide for front-desk and field staff. No technical background needed.

## Signing in

Open the app link on your phone or computer. Sign in with the email and password given to you by your manager. If you don't have an account yet, ask your manager to create one for you — the app doesn't let staff sign themselves up.

Add the app to your home screen for the best experience: on Android Chrome, tap the menu (⋮) → **Add to Home screen**. It then opens like a normal app, works faster, and still shows your task list and tickets even with no signal — a banner at the top says **"You're offline"** when that's happening.

## Sell a WiFi voucher

1. Go to **WiFi → Vouchers**.
2. Tap **Sell Voucher**.
3. Scan or type the voucher code.
4. Search for the customer by phone number, or tap **Skip — anonymous sale** for a walk-in.
5. Confirm the price and payment method (cash, mobile money, etc.) and tap **Sell**.
6. A receipt appears — tap **Print** for the thermal printer, or **Send on WhatsApp** to send it straight to the customer's phone.

If a voucher plan is running low, you'll see a stock warning at the top of the Vouchers screen — tell your manager so they can generate a new batch.

## Take a payment

1. Open the customer's profile (search their name or phone at the top of the app).
2. Go to their **Billing** tab.
3. Tap **Record Payment**.
4. Enter the amount and payment method.
5. Tick which invoice(s) the payment covers — for one invoice this is automatic.
6. Tap **Save**. A receipt appears the same way as a voucher sale.

The customer's running balance on their Billing tab updates immediately.

## Open a support ticket

1. Open the customer's profile, or tap **Tickets → New Ticket** if you're not starting from a customer.
2. Choose a category (e.g. "No Internet", "Slow Speed", "Billing Query") — this sets the priority automatically, but you can change it.
3. Describe the issue and tap **Create**.
4. The ticket shows a countdown to its response and resolution deadlines (based on priority) so you know how urgent it is.

To resolve a ticket: open it, tap **Resolve**, choose a resolution category, and write a short note on what fixed it — this is required and helps the next person who sees a similar issue.

If a customer comes back with the same problem, open the closed ticket and tap **Reopen** — this restarts the clock and keeps the original history on the ticket.

## Renew a subscription

1. Open the customer's profile → **Services** tab (or **WiFi → Subscriptions** and search for them).
2. Tap **Renew**.
3. Choose how many months, confirm the amount, and tap **Renew**.

This automatically extends their expiry date, raises an invoice, and — if you record payment at the same time — allocates the payment against it in one step.

Subscriptions that are about to expire (within 7 days) or have already expired show up automatically as tasks assigned to the account owner, so nothing gets missed even if the customer doesn't call in.

## Logging a call, WhatsApp message, or visit

Tap the **Log Activity** button (top-right on most screens, or press `A` on a keyboard) any time you speak to or visit a customer, even if nothing else changed in the system — this keeps their timeline complete for whoever helps them next.

## Getting help

If something looks wrong (a screen won't load, a number looks incorrect, an action fails with an error), tell your manager or admin rather than trying workarounds — errors are logged automatically and can be looked into.
